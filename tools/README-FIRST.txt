MographJailed - how to install (2 minutes)

1. Double-click  "Install MographJailed.command"  and follow the prompts.

   If your Mac says it can't be opened because it is from the internet:
     open  System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click  "Open Anyway"
     next to the message about "Install MographJailed.command"; then double-click it again.
   (This is macOS being careful about files from a download; it needs no password.)

   Prefer Terminal?  Open Terminal, type  zsh  then a space, drag "Install MographJailed.command"
   into the window, and press Return.

2. When it finishes, open a NEW Terminal window and type:   mj
   First time? type:   mj setup

What it does: copies MographJailed into your Documents folder, adds one line to your Terminal startup
file (a backup is made first), and asks where you keep your projects. No password, no internet, nothing
outside your home folder. It never changes your projects.

To remove it: double-click "Uninstall MographJailed.command". Your projects, versions and reports stay.
